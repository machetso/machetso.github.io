Privacy Policy – My Smartphone Media Backup

Last updated: 5 November 2026

My Smartphone Media Backup (“the app”) is a desktop program that runs entirely on your own computer, together with a small companion app on your Android phone. There is no server operated by the developer. The app contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Data on your phone and computer

The app reads the photos and videos you select on your phone and copies them to drives and folders that you choose. This happens only between your phone and your computer (by USB cable or in your own Wi-Fi network). Settings, backup indexes and reports are stored only on your computer and on your backup drives. None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to add Google Drive as a backup storage, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests only one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file – access to the files and folders that the app itself creates in your Google Drive.

With this permission the app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive. The app uses it only to:

  • create the folder “My Smartphone Media Backup” in your Drive,
  • upload copies of your backed-up photos and videos and the backup reports into that folder,
  • list and check these uploaded files (e.g. compare checksums to verify each upload, skip files that are already there) and show your free Drive space and account e-mail address in the app.

The app communicates directly between your computer and Google. Your files and your Google data are never sent to the developer or to any third party. The sign-in token is stored only on your computer in the app's settings folder (Linux: ~/.config/phonebackup/, Windows: %APPDATA%\PhoneBackup\), readable only by your user account.

You can disconnect at any time with “Sign out” in the app, which deletes the token and revokes the access, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the backup features described above, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about anyone.
